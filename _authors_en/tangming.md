---

Another major interest is the study of traditional Chinese culture and the wisdom of the ancients.

Contemporary education is mostly an education in knowledge.

Knowledge can create wealth and keep a person alive, but it rarely settles the mind.

Reading the classics is at once a way to calm the mind, to cultivate it, and to set one's aims.

In matters of no consequence, keep the openness of a child and a state of ancient simplicity.

When something moves me, I write poetry, to taste the beauty of language.

I hold writing in awe: words carry feeling, and words carry the way.

In short: act rationally when there is work to do, and keep a natural, unhurried state when there is not.

### Attitude to work and life

Engaged in the world for work; detached from it for living.

Do one's utmost to help build a good society,

and take the sky as a mat and the ground as a seat to taste the natural world.

---

High efficiency at work, slow living outside it.

In spare time: books, films, growing vegetables, cooking — and taking pleasure in it all.
